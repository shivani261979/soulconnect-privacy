SoulConnect Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026 Effective date: October 6, 2026

1. Who We Are

SoulConnect is operated by Shivani Jha, located in New York, United States.

SoulConnect is a Sanatan Dharma companion app for scripture reading, daily spiritual reflection, Sadhana, Family Circle activities, parent-guided children's activities, Panchang information, and optional on-device spiritual guidance.

For privacy questions or requests, contact: mumukshu.aum@gmail.com

2. Our Privacy Approach

SoulConnect is designed to be local-first.

Most information you create in SoulConnect stays on your iPhone or iPad. SoulConnect does not require a user account, and the current version does not operate a developer-controlled cloud database for your personal app data.

Limited information may leave your device only when you:

  • Submit feedback through the app;
  • Make or restore a SoulConnect Plus purchase through Apple;
  • Use an Apple service required for a feature, such as location or geocoding;
  • Open an external website;
  • Export information; or
  • Intentionally share content using Apple's share sheet, Mail, Messages, or another service you select.

SoulConnect does not:

  • Display third-party advertising;
  • Use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs;
  • Sell or rent personal information;
  • Share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising;
  • Require an account or sign-in;
  • Operate a remote analytics service;
  • Operate a remote crash-reporting service; or
  • Create public profiles for adults or children.

4. Cross-Device Sync and Device Backups

The current version of SoulConnect does not synchronize app-created information between devices.

SoulConnect does not currently use CloudKit, iCloud Drive, or another developer-controlled cloud database to synchronize:

  • Bookmarks;
  • Notes;
  • Reflections;
  • Quotes;
  • Sadhana history;
  • Family Circle information;
  • Kids Corner information;
  • Blessings;
  • Trivia scores; or
  • Family Tree information.

Your Apple device may nevertheless include SoulConnect app data in an iCloud Backup or a computer backup, depending on your Apple device and backup settings. Apple states that iCloud Backup can include data from downloaded apps that is not otherwise synchronized to iCloud.

These backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by SoulConnect.

Deleting SoulConnect removes the app's local data from that device, but it may not remove information contained in an existing iCloud, Finder, or computer backup. You can manage backups through your Apple device settings.

5. Photos

A parent or adult may choose a photo from the device's photo library for a Family Tree or family-member profile.

The selected app copy:

  • Is stored locally within SoulConnect;
  • Is not automatically uploaded by SoulConnect;
  • Is not included in SoulConnect's current cross-device synchronization, because no such synchronization is enabled; and
  • May leave the device only when the user intentionally exports or shares it.

Deleting a photo from SoulConnect does not delete the original photo from the device's Photos library.

Depending on your device settings, locally stored app photos may be included in a device backup.

6. Location and Panchang

Location access is optional.

If you allow location access, SoulConnect uses the location accuracy you permit in iOS Settings to calculate information such as:

  • Sunrise;
  • Sunset; and
  • Daily Panchang information for your area.

Panchang calculations are performed locally on your device.

Apple's location and geocoding services may process location information to provide a place name under Apple's own privacy practices.

SoulConnect does not:

  • Receive your location on a SoulConnect-operated server;
  • Create a location history;
  • Use location for advertising;
  • Use location for tracking; or
  • Share your location with other SoulConnect users.

You can change or revoke SoulConnect's location permission at any time in: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → SoulConnect

If a manual-location option is available in your version of the app, you may use it instead of granting device-location access.

7. Notifications

SoulConnect schedules reminders locally through Apple's notification system.

The current version does not:

  • Register for remote push notifications;
  • Use a SoulConnect push-notification server; or
  • Automatically send your activity information to another user.

Notification content may appear on your Lock Screen depending on your device settings.

You can modify or disable notification permission through SoulConnect or in: iOS Settings → Notifications → SoulConnect

8. Soul Guide

On supported devices, Soul Guide uses Apple's on-device SystemLanguageModel.

Apple describes SystemLanguageModel as an on-device Apple Foundation Model used for text-generation tasks.

When you use Soul Guide:

  • Your prompt is processed on your device;
  • The generated response is processed on your device;
  • Your prompt and generated response are not transmitted to SoulConnect;
  • SoulConnect does not remotely log your Soul Guide conversation;
  • SoulConnect does not use your prompt for advertising or profiling; and
  • Verses and teachings used by Soul Guide come from content bundled with the app.

If you choose to save a Soul Guide reflection, the saved copy is stored locally in SoulConnect and remains there until you delete it or remove the app, subject to the device-backup information described above.

Soul Guide is intended for spiritual reflection and education. Do not enter confidential information, medical records, financial information, passwords, or emergency information.

Soul Guide may produce incomplete or inaccurate language. Generated reflection should not be treated as scripture, a verified quotation, medical advice, psychological advice, legal advice, or professional advice.

9. Feedback and Support

Feedback is optional.

When you submit feedback through SoulConnect, the app sends the following information to Formspree:

  • Feedback category;
  • Subject; and
  • Message.

The feedback form does not include file attachments.

Formspree accepts, stores, and processes form submissions. According to Formspree's privacy policy, Formspree may automatically process technical information such as an IP address, browser or device information, access time, and related request information. Formspree may process information in the United States and other countries.

Formspree delivers the feedback submission to our email provider. As a result, feedback may be retained by:

  • Formspree;
  • Our email provider; and
  • Us.

We use feedback only to:

  • Respond to your request;
  • Investigate a problem;
  • Provide support;
  • Improve SoulConnect; and
  • Maintain app security and reliability.

We do not use feedback to:

  • Send advertising;
  • Add you to a marketing mailing list;
  • Build an advertising profile; or
  • Sell your information.

Please do not include:

  • Medical or mental-health information;
  • Private journal entries;
  • Passwords;
  • Financial information;
  • A child's personal information;
  • A child's photograph;
  • Precise location information; or
  • Other sensitive personal information.

We generally retain feedback for up to 12 months after the related request is closed, unless it is reasonably necessary to retain it longer for an active support issue, security investigation, legal requirement, or dispute.

To request deletion of feedback, email: mumukshu.aum@gmail.com

Include enough information for us to identify the submission, such as the approximate submission date and subject. Do not send additional sensitive information to verify your request.

Mail Fallback

If the app offers an option to send feedback using your Mail app, your email address, message, and any other information you choose to include will be processed by your selected email provider and our email provider under their respective privacy practices.

SoulConnect Plus purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple using StoreKit.

SoulConnect receives limited transaction or entitlement information from Apple so the app can:

  • Unlock SoulConnect Plus features;
  • Restore a purchase; and
  • Determine whether a subscription remains active.

SoulConnect does not receive or store your complete:

  • Payment-card number;
  • Bank-account number;
  • Apple Account password; or
  • App Store payment credentials.

SoulConnect does not currently use a developer-operated purchase-verification server.

Apple processes payments, transaction records, refunds, subscription management, and billing information under Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

You can manage or cancel a subscription through the Apple subscription settings associated with the purchaser's Apple Account.

SoulConnect allows you to intentionally export or share selected content, including:

  • Reflections;
  • Family Circle information;
  • Family Tree images;
  • Blessings;

  • A child's age band;

Sharing actions in child-facing areas should be performed by a parent or other authorized adult.

12. Family Circle and Kids Corner

Family Circle and Kids Corner are designed for parent-guided family use.

In the current version:

  • Family Circle information remains local;
  • Kids Corner information remains local;
  • Blessings remain local unless intentionally shared;
  • Trivia results remain local unless intentionally exported;
  • Family-member profiles are not public;
  • Children cannot be discovered by strangers;
  • SoulConnect does not operate a public children's feed;
  • SoulConnect does not provide stranger messaging;
  • SoulConnect does not provide child-to-child private messaging; and
  • Family Circle information is not automatically transmitted to relatives on another device.

Parent and adult settings are placed behind a grown-ups-only check where applicable.

Parents can delete child profiles and related local information through the app.

A grown-ups-only check is intended to separate adult controls from child-facing content. It should not be understood as a substitute for any legally required parental-consent process if a future version begins collecting children's information online.

SoulConnect does not ask children to provide:

  • An email address;
  • A phone number;
  • A home address;
  • A school name;
  • A contact list;
  • A full birth date; or
  • Precise location information.

Feedback, purchases, external links, exports, and sharing actions in child-facing areas are intended to be placed behind a grown-ups-only check.

If we learn that a feedback submission contains personal information submitted directly by a child under 13, we will delete it promptly after any necessary one-time response.

COPPA generally requires parental notice and verifiable consent when a child-directed online service collects personal information online from a child under 13. SoulConnect's current child profiles are designed to remain local rather than being collected by us.
